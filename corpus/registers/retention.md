# What is kept, and for how long

## The published record

Organisation names, categories, quantities, reasons and skips. Published to a
public address, kept indefinitely, and that is the point: a funder asking in
March what happened in September gets an answer without anybody searching a
phone.

## What never enters it

No person. No name, address, phone number, email or national identifier of
anyone who collected, drove, cooked or received. Not in a note, not in a reason,
not in a quantity field, not as an example.

This is not a preference. The record cannot be recalled once published, so a
person who enters it stays in it. The gate refuses a draft carrying any of
these shapes and the refusal is not overridable by an approver.

## The provenance thread

Kept for six years, which is the funder's audit window. It carries the
organisation names and the fleet's reasoning. It is not published; it is served
to members of the network.

## Beneficiary counts

Counts are kept. Counts are not people. "41 households" is a count and is
publishable; "41 households including the Georgiou family" is not.
